Route

Drag a stop past the one above or below it to reorder — or use ▲▼. 🔒 marks a committed (hard) time, ☁️ a flexible (soft) time slot. Tap ✏️ to edit a job's schedule.

ⓘ What do these buttons do?
▲ ▼ Move up / down — moves the stop one place earlier or later.
✋ Drag — hold and drag to move a stop several places at once.
✏️ Edit — changes the job itself (time, Hard/Soft, duration, address). The visit order stays the same.
🗑️ Remove — takes the stop off this route. The job is kept on this date and listed under the route as “not on this route”.
After any of these, the whole day is re-timed and the phone map link is updated automatically — you don't need to route again (re-routing starts over and undoes your changes).
Happy with it? Press 📧 Email Approved Route Now to email this route and its link. ✅ Approve copies the planned times onto the Soft jobs. Route Selected Date / Run AI Route rebuild the whole day from scratch.
