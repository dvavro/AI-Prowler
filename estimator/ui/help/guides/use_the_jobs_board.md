# Use the Jobs board
<!-- screen: jobs; words: jobs board, director, waiting, lane, status, next step, mine -->
The Jobs board shows every job the Estimating Director is running — from the web app, the phone or chat — and who each one is waiting on.

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **📋 Jobs board**.
2. Jobs are grouped by lane: **Quick quotes**, **Standard estimates**, **Plan takeoffs**, and **Needs a decision** when the Director needs you to pick a service.
3. **Waiting on** says who's next: you, an approver, office staff or the customer.
4. Tick **Mine only** to see your own jobs, or **Show finished** to include finished ones.
5. Click a job to see its **Next step** and do it there.

## Good to know
- The board updates on its own when someone (or your AI) moves a job along.
- Jobs started in chat with your AI show up here too, and the other way round.
