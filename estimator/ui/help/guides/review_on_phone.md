# Check symbols and measurements on your phone
<!-- screen: reviews; words: phone, tablet, mobile, Jobs app, Estimates tab, review queue, swipe, approve, take out, relabel, skip, measurements, symbols, touch -->
Symbols the plan engine counted and measurements proposed from the plans wait for a person before they're priced. You can check them one at a time on a phone or tablet, wherever you are.

1. In the AI-Prowler Jobs app, open the **Estimates** tab and tap **Reviews**. The number beside it is how many are waiting.
2. The least sure item is first. The picture shows it in place on its sheet, outlined in red, with the project and sheet below.
3. Tap **✓ Approve** if it's right, or **✗ Take out** if it isn't, then **Yes**. You can also swipe the picture right to approve or left to take out (it still asks first). The next item comes up by itself.
4. A symbol counted as the wrong thing, or not named at all: tap **Relabel…**, choose what it is, tick **Every identical match on this plan set** if you want, then **Relabel…** and **Yes**.
5. Not sure? Tap **Skip ▶** to come back to it later, or **Open on the plan (desktop)** to look at it in Blueprint Review.
6. **Symbols** and **Measurements** at the top show just one kind; the project list shows one project.

Drawing new shapes and setting scales stay on the desktop, where it's easier to be precise. Measurements that were typed in have no picture. A measurement with a suggested change says so: check it on the desktop.
Field staff can look at the symbols; an owner, estimator or reviewer approves them.

Ask your AI: "what's still waiting for review on the Smith plans?", "approve measurement 52".
