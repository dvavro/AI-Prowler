# Use the dashboard
<!-- screen: dashboard; words: dashboard, home, quick estimate, jobs board, attention, estimates, numbers, tiles, recent -->

The dashboard is home: what needs your attention, your estimate numbers, and the two fastest ways to start work.

1. **⚡ Quick estimate** starts a quote in two minutes — no plans needed. Pick a service, answer the questions, and the price works itself out from your Price Book.
2. **📋 Jobs board** shows every job the Director is running, and who each one is waiting for.
3. **Needs attention**: estimates waiting for review, and work assigned to you, come first.
4. Click a number in the tiles to see every estimate with that status — draft, approved, sent, won or lost.
5. **Recent** lists the estimates changed most recently. Click one to open its project.

## Good to know

- Not sure where to start? **🧭 AI Director Agent — get estimate started** asks what the job is, whether you have plans and who it's for, then takes you to the right page and guides every step to sending the quote.
- The dashboard shows numbers, not actions: nothing here changes an estimate.
- If a tile is empty, the list it opens is empty too — start from **⚡ Quick estimate** or **Create project** on Projects.
- Ask your AI: "what needs my attention?", "how many estimates are out?", "show me the Miller estimate".
