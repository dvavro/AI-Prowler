# Let the AI design it (✨ AI design)

Have a shed, shop or house drawing with no electrical on it? **✨ AI design** on the Measure screen lets you say what you want in plain words — the AI reads the building, places every symbol to the code rules and your specs, and draws a new electrical plan and panel schedule. Nothing is priced until you approve it. It runs on the agent API key (turn **AI measuring** on in 🤖 AI agents). **No plans at all?** Describe the job on the project page and the AI draws the plans for every trade — see below.

1. Upload the building's drawings on the project page (a PDF, a photo or a CAD file) and read them (📖).
2. On Measure, click **✨ AI design** on the toolbar, beside 🤖 AI Auto Measure/Count. It opens in its own window (close it with ✕ or Esc; what you typed is kept).
3. Pick **Electrical** and the drawings to design on, and say what you want, for example: *"Here's my shed. Add electricity: LED shop lighting, 120 V receptacles on 20 A circuits, one 240 V 30 A receptacle and a 100 A subpanel fed underground from the house garage's 100 A subpanel."*
4. Add your specs if you have any (receptacle height, fixture type, conduit or cable…) under **Specs and preferences**, then click **✨ Design it**. It takes a minute or two; you can close the window and keep working — the toolbar button says **⏳ AI design working…**, then **(ready to review)**, and a message pops up when it's done.
5. Look at it: **👁 View the plan** and **👁 Panel schedule** (scroll to zoom, drag to move, ⬇ Download DXF). The card lists what it added, the circuits, the AI's assumptions and the code check. **The new design is the drawing on the Measure screen right away** — the plan and panel schedule are put on the project, opened first, and every device is counted, waiting for your review. A note says it isn't approved yet; your own older electrical drawings stay until you approve.
6. Want something different? Type it under **Change it in plain words** — *"add two outlets on the rear wall"*, *"move the panel to the right wall"*, *"put the lights on two circuits"* — and click **✏️ Make the change**. The card shows what changed, and the changed drawing replaces it on Measure right away. **🗑 Discard** forgets a change you haven't approved and puts the approved design back.
   - **Show it where**: click **📎 Add screenshot** (or paste one into the box with Ctrl+V) — up to 3 pictures. Draw arrows, circles or notes on them (*"move this here"*, *"two more like this along this wall"*). The AI also gets the app's **🔢 Numbered plan**: the building on a 5 ft grid with every device numbered, so it can match your marks to real positions in feet.
   - Or say it by number: open **🔢 Numbered plan** and type *"move device 12 to x 30, y 44"* (x runs along the front wall from the front-left corner, y toward the rear). **⬇ Save picture** saves it as a PNG to mark up.
7. Click **✅ Approve design** and say yes to the question — it lists exactly what changes. That's the only click: the app does the rest in a few seconds (no AI cost), and the window shows each step:
   - the design's plan and panel schedule **replace the older electrical drawings** on the project (any plan set whose sheets are all electrical — drawn by hand, uploaded, or an earlier AI design) together with their measurements, the same way a new plan revision replaces the old one. The architectural plans stay;
   - any other electrical count left on the estimate (for example one measured on an architectural sheet) is set aside as rejected, with the reason — it stays in the history;
   - the device counts from the new drawing are approved — the drawing is taken as the latest, so a count it changed takes its new number and a device that's no longer on it is taken off and priced as none — and the **wiring run** routes every circuit and measures the cable;
   - those counts and the cable are approved (they come from the design you approved) and the estimate's **Electrical is re-priced**. If the estimate is already approved or sent, it waits for you: click **🔄 Update from the plans** on the estimate (that reopens it).
   If a step can't finish, the design stays approved and the window says what to do by hand.

## No plans at all? Describe the job and the AI draws them — or just prices it
On the project page, open **✨ AI design: describe the job** (under Plans and documents — the Director's *"No plans?"* step takes you straight there). It works for every trade, not only electrical:

1. Say what the job is in your own words — sizes, materials, where things go. For example: *"Back yard of a 100 x 150 lot behind a 40 x 30 house: 6 ft wood privacy fence on three sides from the house's back corners, a 4 ft walk gate and a 12 ft double drive gate, St. Augustine sod in the back yard."* Or: *"A 20 x 30 shed with a shop and a half bath, 4 in slab, gable roof at 4:12, a 10 x 10 roll-up door, a 36 in entry door and three windows."* Or: *"Gut the 10 x 20 kitchen to the studs — 25 ft of cabinets and the sink come out."* Add specs if you have any, pick **what AI design should do**, then click **✨ Run AI design**. It takes a minute or two; you can leave the page.
   - **📐 Draw the plans** — draw the sheets and put them on the Measure screen, where every trade they show is measured (steps 2–3 below). On by default — off for a job no drawing helps, like **demolition only**.
   - **💲 Price the estimate** — fill in each of the project's trades from your description (square feet, counts, choices — the same questions you'd answer on the estimate). The card shows what it filled in under **Fills in the estimate**; approving prices it. A trade the drawing measures is priced from the drawing; the description fills in the rest (demolition, roofing…). An answer a trade can't take (a choice it doesn't have, a number out of range) is left out and said in the check — never guessed.
   - **📋 Include permits and inspections** — answer each trade's permit and inspection questions as the job needs them. Off: they stay as the estimate has them.
   - With **Draw the plans** off, nothing is drawn: the button says **✅ Approve and price**, and approving fills in and prices the trades. The same choices are under **Change it in plain words**, so a change can turn either one on or off.
   - **Show it where**: paste a screen clip into the box (Ctrl+V) or click **📎 Add screenshot** — up to 3 pictures: an aerial or satellite view, a survey or plot plan, a photo of the yard, a clip of a drawing. Draw on them first (*"fence here"*, a line along the property, an arrow to the gate). The AI lays the job out the way the pictures show; sizes come from your description first, then from dimensions or a scale on the picture, then from things of known size in it — its assumptions say which sizes it estimated. A change can bring its own screenshots too.
2. The app draws the sheets the job needs — **C-101 Site plan** (fences, gates, sod and planting areas, patios and driveways), **A-101 Floor plan** (walls, rooms, doors, windows, fixtures, with door, window and room finish schedules), **S-101 Foundation plan**, **A-201 Roof plan** and **D-101 Demolition plan**. Click a sheet to look at it (⬇ Download DXF for any CAD program). The card counts what's on them and runs a check (a gate that isn't on a fence, a door off its wall, a room outside the building…).
3. **The newest drawing is always the one on the Measure screen.** As soon as it's drawn, its sheets become the project's plans (in place of the ones the design drew before), the plan readers read them like any designer's drawings, and every trade they answer (fencing, landscaping, framing, drywall, painting, insulation, flooring, windows and doors, siding, HVAC, plumbing, electrical) is measured — as proposals waiting for you. The picker on Measure says **AI design revision N**, the same number as this card, and a note says while it isn't approved yet.
4. Change it in plain words — *"make the fence 8 ft tall"*, *"move the drive gate to the left side"* — and click **✏️ Make the change**. The changed drawing replaces it on Measure right away.
   - **What a fence site plan shows:** every note the AI wrote, in full; each gate marked **G1**, **G2** … with its post marks and a dimension from the end of its fence run (the house side) to the opening, which updates whenever a gate moves; **LATCH** and **HINGE** at its posts and its swing drawn open, when the AI was told the latch side and which way it opens (a pool gate opens outward, away from the pool); and a **FENCE AND GATE SCHEDULE** — each run's length, height, material and style and each gate's size, with the **maker, model, panel size, color, hinges and latch the customer picked**. What hasn't been picked says **TO BE SELECTED** (the AI never makes up a brand or model) — say it in plain words, e.g. *"use Acme Pool Pro 200 black, 54 in x 6 ft panels, MagnaLatch top-pull latches"*, and the schedule is what you order from.
5. Click **✅ Approve this drawing** and say yes. Its measurements are approved and the estimate's prices are updated — what isn't a quantity (the fence's material and height, for example) comes from the plans too. The drawing is taken as the latest: what you'd approved that it measures differently takes its new number, and anything that isn't on it any more (a drive gate you took out) is taken off and priced as none. (**Keep approved items on rerun** is for 🤖 AI Auto Measure/Count reruns, not AI design.) **Concrete** is measured from the design's own foundation plan and flatwork — slab and pour areas, edges, thickened edge and thickness — with no AI cost. Tick **Approve each new design automatically** to skip this step — each design is approved as soon as it's ready. **🗑 Discard** forgets a drawing you haven't approved and puts the approved one back on Measure. If the estimate is already approved or sent, its prices wait for you: open the estimate and click **🔄 Update from the plans** — it reopens the estimate and takes the plans' quantities, material and height (for example Wrought iron → Aluminum, 4 ft → 4 ft 6 in). With **Price the estimate** on, roofing and demolition are filled in from your description; otherwise they're measured by **🤖 AI Auto Measure/Count** on Measure from their sheets — the card tells you when.
6. Approving again after more changes prices again.

Rather type the numbers yourself? Skip the plans: on the project page click **⚡ Add a quick estimate** (or the Director's step for each trade) and type the measurements into each trade.

## Good to know
- **The code check**: GFCI where it's required, receptacle spacing, breaker and wire sizes, how full each circuit is, switched lighting in every room, smoke alarms, a 240 V load on a 2-pole breaker, the panel and feeder sizes, and anything outside the building. ⛔ errors must be fixed before approving (ask: *"fix the errors"*); ⚠️ warnings are things to look at. When the first design has errors, the AI gets one turn to fix them on its own.
- **The AI places every symbol**; the app only sets wall devices 0.7 ft off their wall, facing it, and slides one out of a doorway — the code check notes each such move.
- Each symbol on the drawing carries its circuit, so ⚡ Run wiring puts every device on the circuit the design chose.
- From a PDF or photo, the AI reads the building's walls, doors, windows and rooms from the written dimensions first; from a CAD file, the walls are read directly (no AI cost for that step). Check the building line on the card (size, kind, where it was read from).
- Approving again after more changes replaces the plan sets the last approval made, and measures and prices again. A design approved before this worked automatically shows **✅ Put it in place of the older drawings** — click it once to do the rest. **🗑 Discard** forgets a proposed design — nothing on the estimate changes. **＋ New design** starts over.
- Every AI call is in the AI Log and counts against the AI budget; the card shows what the design has cost so far.
- It's a design for estimating: a licensed electrician and the local authority confirm the final layout. Electrical is ready now; plumbing, HVAC and low voltage are coming.
