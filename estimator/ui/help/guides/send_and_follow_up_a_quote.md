# Send and follow up a quote
<!-- screen: jobs; words: send, email, approve, bid, quote, customer, follow up, won, lost, jobs board, included, not included, scope, bid writer, revised quote, changes, reopen -->
After a job is priced, the Director walks you through approving it, sending it and hearing back from the customer.

1. Open the **📋 Jobs board** from the Dashboard and click the job (or click **Next: approve and send →** after pricing).
2. Read the **Next step** box. If it says the price uses sample prices, tick **Use the sample prices anyway** only if you're sure — or set your prices first.
3. Click **✓ Approve**. Small quick quotes can be approved by anyone who quotes; bigger ones need an owner or reviewer.
4. The quote is made for you. If the **Bid Writer** agent is on, it first writes the scope and the **Included** / **Not included** lists for the quote. If it runs from chat, the Director waits and says so — ask your AI to do the next agent task, or make the quote without the lists.
5. Type the customer's email under **Send to** and click **📨 Send the quote…**.
6. Check the question (who it goes to and the total) and click **Yes**. Nothing is emailed until you do.
7. When the customer answers, open the job again and click **👍 Customer said yes** or **Customer said no**.

## The customer wants changes
- **Before they've said yes:** open the estimate and click **✏️ Reopen for changes…**. Change what's needed; the next approval shows only what changed. The new quote (for example Q-0006-2) tells the customer which quote it replaces and what changed, and the old one is voided.
- **After they've said yes:** click **➕ Change order…** on the estimate instead (see "Turn a won quote into a job").

## Good to know
- To see the quote exactly as the customer will, open it from the job (**Open the quote**) or from the **Bids** screen.
- The quote is emailed through AI-Prowler's email setup, with the PDF attached when it could be made.
- Anything the estimate prices is never listed as **Not included** — if the Bid Writer lists it, it's taken off the list and you're told.
- The Director reminds you when a sent quote is due a follow-up.
