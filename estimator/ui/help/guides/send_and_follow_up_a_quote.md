# Send and follow up a quote
<!-- screen: jobs; words: send, email, approve, bid, quote, customer, follow up, won, lost, jobs board -->
After a job is priced, the Director walks you through approving it, sending it and hearing back from the customer.

1. Open the **📋 Jobs board** from the Dashboard and click the job (or click **Next: approve and send →** after pricing).
2. Read the **Next step** box. If it says the price uses sample prices, tick **Use the sample prices anyway** only if you're sure — or set your prices first.
3. Click **✓ Approve**. Small quick quotes can be approved by anyone who quotes; bigger ones need an owner or reviewer.
4. The quote is made for you. Type the customer's email under **Send to** and click **📨 Send the quote…**.
5. Check the question (who it goes to and the total) and click **Yes**. Nothing is emailed until you do.
6. When the customer answers, open the job again and click **👍 Customer said yes** or **Customer said no**.

## Good to know
- To see the quote exactly as the customer will, open it from the job (**Open the quote**) or from the **Bids** screen.
- The quote is emailed through AI-Prowler's email setup, with the PDF attached when it could be made.
- The Director reminds you when a sent quote is due a follow-up.
