# Set up the trade agents and watch takeoff runs
<!-- screen: agents; words: agent manager, agents, trade agents, takeoff run, runner, turn off a trade, confidence, auto approve, retry, failed, blocked -->
Each construction trade has a takeoff agent that measures its quantities from the plans. The Agents screen is where the owner sets them up and where you watch their runs.

1. Open **Agents**. Each row is a trade.
2. Untick **On** to stop a trade being measured (it's skipped in new runs).
3. **Sure enough at** is how sure the agent must be (0–100%) before its quantities can go through.
4. **Approve by itself** lets confident quantities be priced without a person. It's off at first; leave it off until you trust that trade.
5. Click **Save…** on the row, then **Yes**. Only the owner can change these.
6. Under **Takeoff runs**, click a run to see each trade: waiting, measuring, waiting for a person, done, blocked or failed, and what happens next.
7. A failed or blocked trade has **Try again…** (owner and estimator).

The Director starts a run for each plan job. Trades wait for the ones they depend on (drywall, painting and insulation wait for framing).

Ask your AI: "turn off the landscaping agent", "how's the takeoff run on the Smith job going?", "retry the framing takeoff".
