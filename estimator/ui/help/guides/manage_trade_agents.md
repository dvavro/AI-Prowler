# Choose your trades and watch takeoff runs
<!-- screen: agents; words: agent manager, agents, trade agents, trades, turn off a trade, switch off, takeoff run, runner, confidence, sure enough, auto approve, retry, failed, blocked -->
Which trades you quote is set once, on the desktop. The Agents screen in the web app is where you watch the takeoff runs.

1. On the PC, open AI-Prowler's **🏗 Estimator** tab and go to **🏗 Trades**.
2. Untick a trade you don't do. It's then not offered anywhere in the web app (trade pickers, Price Book, plan symbols), the AI takeoff doesn't measure it and the Director leaves it out. Estimates that already have it keep working.
3. **Sure enough at** is how sure that trade's takeoff agent must be (0–100%) before its quantities can go through.
4. **Approve by itself** lets confident quantities be priced without a person. It's off at first; leave it off until you trust that trade.
5. Click **💾 Save takeoff settings**. (The trade on/off boxes save as soon as you click them.)
6. In the web app, open **Agents**. Under **Takeoff runs**, click a run to see each trade: waiting, measuring, waiting for a person, done, blocked or failed, and what happens next.
7. A failed or blocked trade has **Try again…** (owner and estimator).

The Director starts a run for each plan job. Trades wait for the ones they depend on (drywall, painting and insulation wait for framing). Services (washing, cleaning, pools and the like) are switched on and off the same way, under **Services** in the same section.

Ask your AI: "how's the takeoff run on the Smith job going?", "retry the framing takeoff".
