# Choose your trades and watch measure runs
<!-- screen: agents; words: agent manager, agents, trade agents, trades, turn off a trade, switch off, takeoff run, runner, confidence, sure enough, auto approve, retry, failed, blocked, ai auto run, auto runs, measure runs, cost -->
Which trades you quote is set once, on the desktop. The Agents screen in the web app is where you watch the measure runs.

1. On the PC, open AI-Prowler's **🏗 Estimator** tab and go to **🏗 Trades**.
2. Untick a trade you don't do. It's then not offered anywhere in the web app (trade pickers, Price Book, plan symbols), the AI doesn't measure it and the Director leaves it out. Estimates that already have it keep working.
3. **Sure enough at** is how sure that trade's measuring agent must be (0–100%) before its quantities can go through.
4. **Approve by itself** lets confident quantities be priced without a person. It's off at first; leave it off until you trust that trade.
5. Click **💾 Save agent settings**. (The trade on/off boxes save as soon as you click them.)
6. In the web app, open **Agents**. **🤖 AI Auto Measure/Count runs** lists every run of the AI Auto Measure/Count button on Measure, for every trade: the project, how it went, what it proposed, what its AI calls cost and, if it failed, why. **Open its Measure page** goes to the run; **📋 Its AI calls** shows what was sent to the AI and what came back.
7. Under **Director measure runs**, click a run to see each trade: waiting, measuring, waiting for a person, done, blocked or failed, and what happens next. A failed or blocked trade has **Try again…** (owner and estimator).

The Director starts a Director measure run for each plan job. Trades wait for the ones they depend on (drywall, painting and insulation wait for framing). Services (washing, cleaning, pools and the like) are switched on and off the same way, under **Services** in the same section.

Ask your AI: "how's the measure run on the Smith job going?", "retry the framing measure run".
