# Change your settings
<!-- screen: settings; words: settings, company, logo, tax, markup, overhead, profit, labor rate, bid template, terms, approval, privacy, backup, audit -->
Settings is where the owner changes the company details and the rules every estimate and bid follows.

1. Open **Settings** in the left menu (owner only).
2. **🏢 Company**: name, address, phone, license number and the logo file printed on your bids.
3. **💲 Tax, markups and labor**: the sales tax rate and what it's charged on; overhead, profit and labor burden (as percents); the minimum charge and rounding; and your labor rates.
4. **📨 Bid template**: how long a quote is valid, whether each line or one price per trade is shown, the terms, and what's always included or never included.
5. **✅ Approval rules**: the amount over which a reviewer must sign off, the quick-quote limit that sends with one tap, and the follow-up and reminder days.
6. **🤖 AI and privacy**: turn symbol-crop sending on or off and see how much has been sent.
7. **👥 Users and roles**, **💾 Data and backups** (with **Back up now…**) and **📜 Audit log** (every change, newest first).
8. Each **Save…** lists every change (old → new) and saves when you click **Yes**.

## Good to know
- New settings apply to new and re-priced estimates; bids already made keep their numbers.
- People and roles come from AI-Prowler's own accounts (Admin, Users and Manage Scopes).
- Restoring a backup and moving the data folder are done on the PC, in the Estimator tab.
