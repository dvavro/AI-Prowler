# Teach AI-Prowler your company's symbols
<!-- screen: symbol_library; words: symbol library, define symbol, company symbols, standard symbols, legend, code, export, import, assembly, learn -->
AI-Prowler knows what a symbol means from three places, in this order: the plan set's own legend, your company's Symbol Library, then AI-Prowler's standard symbols (receptacles, switches, fixtures, diffusers...).

1. Open **Symbol Library**. Pick a **Trade** to see just its symbols.
2. Your company's symbols are learned in **Reviews**: when you approve or relabel a symbol, its shape is saved as an example. The **Learned examples** column shows how many each one has; **Found on your plans** shows how often it was found.
3. To add one yourself, click **New symbol…**, give the item type (e.g. floor_box), a description, the trade and the code written beside it (e.g. FB), and click **Save…** then **Yes**.
4. To change one, click **Edit…** on its row. Saving makes a new version; counts already approved don't change.
5. **Export** saves your company library as a file; **Import…** loads one (for another office, or a backup), then **Yes**.

AI-Prowler's standard symbols can't be edited; add a company symbol with the same item type instead.

**What the columns mean**
- **Picture** — how the symbol is usually drawn on US plan sets. AI-Prowler's standard symbols (electrical, low voltage, plumbing, HVAC, fire, architectural and landscape) come with a drawing installed. They follow the conventions most US plan sets use (NECA 100 for electrical, the National CAD Standard, NFPA 170 for fire), but each design firm can draw them its own way, which is why a plan set's legend always comes first. Once a symbol has been learned from your plans, the picture becomes the real one from your plans.
- **Learned examples** — pictures of the symbol saved from your own plans each time someone approves or relabels one in Reviews. The plan engine looks for these on every new plan set, so the more examples a symbol has, the better it's found.
- **Found on your plans** — how many times the symbol has been counted on your plans so far (ones taken out in Reviews don't count).

Both numbers start at 0 on a new install and grow as you review plans.

Owners, estimators and reviewers use this screen.

Ask your AI: "what symbols does our library have for plumbing?", "save FB as a floor box in our library".
