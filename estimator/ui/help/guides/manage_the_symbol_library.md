# Teach AI-Prowler your company's symbols
<!-- screen: symbol_library; words: symbol library, define symbol, company symbols, standard symbols, legend, code, export, import, assembly, learn -->
AI-Prowler knows what a symbol means from three places, in this order: the plan set's own legend, your company's Symbol Library, then AI-Prowler's standard symbols (receptacles, switches, fixtures, diffusers...).

1. Open **Symbol Library**. Pick a **Trade** to see just its symbols.
2. Your company's symbols are learned in **Reviews**: when you approve or relabel a symbol, its shape is saved as an example. The **Examples** column shows how many each one has; **Found** shows how often it was found.
3. To add one yourself, click **New symbol…**, give the item type (e.g. floor_box), a description, the trade and the code written beside it (e.g. FB), and click **Save…** then **Yes**.
4. To change one, click **Edit…** on its row. Saving makes a new version; counts already approved don't change.
5. **Export** saves your company library as a file; **Import…** loads one (for another office, or a backup), then **Yes**.

AI-Prowler's standard symbols can't be edited; add a company symbol with the same item type instead.
Owners, estimators and reviewers use this screen.

Ask your AI: "what symbols does our library have for plumbing?", "save FB as a floor box in our library".
