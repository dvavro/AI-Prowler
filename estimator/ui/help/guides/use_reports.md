# Use the reports
<!-- screen: reports; words: reports, win, loss, win rate, takeoff report, export, excel, csv, actual, job cost, revision -->
Reports show how your bids are doing, what was measured on each project, and how your prices compare with what jobs really cost.

1. Open **Reports** in the left menu (owners, estimators, reviewers and office).
2. **🏆 Win / loss**: your win rate (won ÷ won + lost), the value won and lost, the bids out now, by month and by trade. Mark bids won or lost on the Bids screen.
3. **📏 Takeoff report**: pick a project to see every measurement by trade — approved (priced) and still waiting, which need re-measuring after a plan revision, and which came from scans or photos. Click one to open it in Blueprint Review.
4. **🔀 Plan revisions**: revised plan sets to compare, and the saved revision reports (see "Compare plan revisions").
5. **💵 Estimate vs actual**: your unit costs against past estimates and what jobs really paid. Pick a project to check each of its lines.
6. **⬇ Exports**: pick a project (its lines and totals) or every estimate (optionally one status), choose Excel or CSV, and click **⬇ Export**. The file downloads and is also kept in the Estimator's exports folder.

## Good to know
- Practice estimates are left out of the win/loss numbers and the job-cost comparison.
- Nothing on this screen changes an estimate, except comparing two plan sets, which asks first.
- Ask your AI: "what's my win rate this year?", "export the Miller estimate to Excel", "where do my prices run high?".
