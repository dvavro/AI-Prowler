# Turn a won quote into a job
<!-- screen: bids; words: won, convert, job, schedule, customer, service date, AI-Prowler, change order, extra work, more work, add to job -->
When the customer says yes, one step puts the work into AI-Prowler: the customer (if new), an approved quote and a job.

1. Open the job on the **📋 Jobs board** (or the quote on the **Bids** screen).
2. If you haven't yet, click **👍 Customer said yes** (on the Bids screen: **👍 Won**).
3. Pick a **Service date** if you know it — the job is then created as Scheduled. Leave it empty to schedule later.
4. Click **Make it a job in AI-Prowler…**, check the question and click **Yes**.
5. The message shows what was created (customer, quote and job numbers). The job is now in the Jobs app like any other.

## The customer wants more or different work (change order)
1. Open the won estimate and click **➕ Change order…**.
2. Type what the customer wants (for example "extend the patio 10 ft") and click **Make the change order…**, then **Yes**.
3. A new estimate opens, named "… — Change order 1". Price the extra or changed work there and approve it.
4. Send its quote. It's headed **Change order** and names the accepted quote it adds to.
5. When the customer accepts, click **👍 Customer said yes**, then **Make it a job in AI-Prowler…** and **Yes**. For a change order this adds it to the job the first quote made (no new job): the job's quote amount goes up by the change order, and the accepted quote stays as signed.

## Good to know
- On the Bids screen you can fill in the **Customer** name first if the quote didn't have one.
- Recurring services (for example monthly window cleaning) carry their schedule onto the job.
- A quote can only become a job once; doing it again just shows the job it already made.
- A won estimate can't be reopened — changes after a yes are always change orders, so the signed quote never changes.
