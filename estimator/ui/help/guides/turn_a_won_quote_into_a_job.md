# Turn a won quote into a job
<!-- screen: bids; words: won, convert, job, schedule, customer, service date, AI-Prowler -->
When the customer says yes, one step puts the work into AI-Prowler: the customer (if new), an approved quote and a job.

1. Open the job on the **📋 Jobs board** (or the quote on the **Bids** screen).
2. If you haven't yet, click **👍 Customer said yes** (on the Bids screen: **👍 Won**).
3. Pick a **Service date** if you know it — the job is then created as Scheduled. Leave it empty to schedule later.
4. Click **Make it a job in AI-Prowler…**, check the question and click **Yes**.
5. The message shows what was created (customer, quote and job numbers). The job is now in the Jobs app like any other.

## Good to know
- On the Bids screen you can fill in the **Customer** name first if the quote didn't have one.
- Recurring services (for example monthly window cleaning) carry their schedule onto the job.
- A quote can only become a job once; doing it again just shows the job it already made.
