# Check the symbol counts before they're priced
<!-- screen: reviews; words: review, verify, approve, reject, take out, relabel, wrong symbol, missed symbol, find similar, thumbnails, counts, detections, outlets -->
Symbols the plan engine counts are proposals until a person checks them. The Reviews screen shows each one as a small picture cut from the plans, so you can check a whole sheet's outlets in a minute.

1. Open **Reviews** and click a plan set. The ones with the most symbols still to check are at the top.
2. Click a type at the top (e.g. **Duplex receptacle**) to see just those. **Sort: Least sure first** puts the doubtful ones first.
3. Click the pictures to select them (or **Select all**). The line above the pictures says how many match and how many are selected.
4. Click **✓ Approve** for the right ones and **✗ Take out** for the wrong ones, then **Yes**. Taken-out symbols stay listed but aren't counted.
5. An unidentified symbol, or one counted as the wrong thing: select it, click **Relabel…**, type what it is (e.g. floor_box), tick **every identical match on this plan set** if you want, and click **Relabel…** then **Yes**.
6. To find more of a symbol the engine missed: select one, click **Find similar…**. It says how many it found on every sheet; **Yes** adds them as "to check".
7. A symbol the engine missed completely: open **Add a symbol the engine missed**, pick the sheet, give where it is and what it is, and click **Add…**.
8. **open on the plan** under a picture opens it in Blueprint Review to see it in place.

Every change keeps the first result in its history ("was … , to check"). Approved, relabelled and added symbols teach your company's Symbol Library, so the next plan set from the same architect finds them by itself.
Field staff can look at the symbols; an owner, estimator or reviewer approves them. AI agents never approve.

Ask your AI: "show me the outlets still to check on the Smith plans", "take out symbol 214", "those three are floor boxes".
