# Quote a service job in 2 minutes
<!-- screen: quick; words: quick, estimate, price, pressure washing, window cleaning, house cleaning, service -->
For service work (pressure washing, window cleaning, house cleaning and the like) you answer a few questions and get a price straight away. No plans needed.

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **⚡ Quick estimate**. (On a phone, open the **Estimates** tab in the AI-Prowler Jobs app.)
2. Under **What's the job?**, choose the service.
3. Answer the questions. The ones marked * are needed; the rest have sensible defaults.
4. Type the job address (and a project name if you like), then click **Price it**.
5. Check the price. A yellow note means it uses **sample prices** — set your own in the Price Book before you send real quotes (see "Set up your price book").
6. Click **Next: approve and send →** to carry on: approve the price, then send the quote to the customer (see "Send and follow up a quote").

## Good to know
- Every quick quote goes on the **📋 Jobs board**, so you can pick it up later from any PC or phone.
- You can also just ask your AI: "quote a 1,000 sq ft driveway wash at 12 Oak St".
- Field crew can give quick quotes; prices are shown but costs and margins are hidden.
