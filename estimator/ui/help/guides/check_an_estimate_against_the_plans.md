# Check an estimate against the plans
<!-- screen: estimates; words: estimate, lines, materials, quantities, measurements, linked, recalculate, check, options, good better best, revision, version, add a line -->
The Estimates screen shows every trade and line on an estimate, and where each quantity came from on the plans.

1. Open **Estimates** and click the estimate (or **🧮 Open the estimate** on the project page).
2. Under each trade, the table at the top says where each answer came from: **📏 measured** (it matches the approved measurements), **⚠ differs from the measurements**, **typed in**, or **📏 measured, not priced yet**.
3. If a trade differs or isn't priced yet, click **Price it with the approved measurements…** and **Yes**. The trade is priced again with the measured totals. Only approved measurements count.
4. In the lines table, click **📏** on a line to see the measurements behind it. Click **show on the plan** for one, or **Show all of them on the plan** to open Blueprint Review with every one of them highlighted.
5. Click **✓ Check it** for the reviewer's checks (errors that block approval, warnings to look at). Owners and estimators can also **Compare with past jobs**.
6. **Good / better / best options**: click **Price the options**, then **Use this one…** to switch the trade to an option.
7. **＋ Add a line by hand** adds a line that doesn't come from a trade (a dumpster, a permit). Give a unit cost or a price book key. Click **Recalculate** after prices or markups change.
8. An estimate that's approved or sent can't be changed. Click **New version…**, say what's changing, and **Yes**. The old version stays as it was and its open quotes are voided.

## Good to know
- In Blueprint Review, a measurement's **Detail** lists the priced lines it feeds, and the **Lines** tab highlights all the measurements behind a line.
- Field staff see prices but never costs or margins.
