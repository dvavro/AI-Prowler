# Work on an estimate
<!-- screen: estimate; words: estimate, lines, edit answers, price it again, total, cost, markups, add a line, edit a line, price book item, reopen, change order, permit, inspection, survey, approved -->
The estimate page shows every trade, its lines and the total. Here you change answers, fix lines and add your own.

1. Open the estimate from **Estimates**, a project page, or the 🧭 Director box.
2. Each trade lists its lines. **Cost** is what the work costs you; **Price (with markups)** is what the customer pays — the small note under it shows the overhead, profit and tax. (Field crew see the price only.)
3. To change a trade's answers (rebar size, vapor barrier, a permit, inspections, a survey…), click **Edit answers…**. Every answer you saved is filled in; change what you need and click **Price it again**.
4. To change one line, click **Edit…** on it. Pick another **Price book item** and every field changes to it; that pick stays when the trade is priced again. If the line's cost no longer matches the price book, **Use the price book's** brings it back in line.
5. For work no trade covers (fill dirt, a dumpster, an extra), open **＋ Add a line by hand**, pick a price book item or type one, and click **Add the line**.
6. **Estimate total** at the bottom adds up every trade and the hand-added lines, then shows the overhead, profit and tax inside the total.

## After it's approved
- **✏️ Reopen for changes…** (approved or sent, not yet won): edit the same estimate. The next approval shows only what changed, and a sent quote is replaced by a new one that says what changed.
- **➕ Change order…** (won): extra or different work after the customer said yes, priced and quoted on its own — see "Turn a won quote into a job".
- **New version…** makes a separate copy (v2) and leaves this one unchanged.

## Good to know
- **Price it again** rebuilds the trade from its answers: a line you took off by hand comes back if its answer still asks for it (for example Reinforcement still says Wire mesh). Change the answer instead.
- Hand-added lines and picked price book items stay when a trade is priced again; other hand changes to a trade's lines are replaced.
- Permits, inspections and surveys are off until you ask for them — set their real prices in the Price Book.
