💡 How-To Tips
⏱
How to Clock In
Click Time Clock in the left sidebar → press the green Clock In button. Press Clock Out when done. Your session is logged automatically.
🏖
How to Request Time Off
Go to PTO & Time Off → fill in dates, select type (Vacation, Sick, etc.), add a note → click Submit Request. HR will approve or decline.
⚡
Running Late or Out Today?
Go to Time Clock and tap Running Late or Out Today — it notifies HR instantly so they know without you needing to call.
💰
How to View Your Pay
Go to Payroll under the Money section to see your pay stubs, rates, and history. Direct deposit info is under Deposits.
💬
How to Message HR
Click Messages in the sidebar to send a private, confidential message directly to HR. You can ask questions, raise concerns, or follow up on anything. HR will reply in the same inbox.
🚨
How to File an Incident Report
Go to Incident Reports under the HR section → click + New Incident Report → fill out the form and submit. It goes directly to HR. All reports are confidential.
📷
How to Update Your Profile Photo
Go to Portfolio in the sidebar → click your avatar → upload a photo. It will appear across the portal.

🏢 About AI-Prowler

AI-Prowler is a self-hosted AI platform that runs on a Windows computer. That means a business's AI assistant and its private knowledge base run on its own machine — your company's documents aren't handed off to someone else's cloud.

It connects to everyday tools, supports multiple users, and powers the apps you're using right now.

📋 What is a Write-Up?

A write-up is a formal written notice from your manager or HR documenting a policy violation, attendance issue, or performance concern. Write-ups are part of AI-Prowler's progressive discipline process and are intended to be constructive — not punitive.

1️⃣
Verbal Warning
An informal conversation with your manager about a concern. Not always documented.
2️⃣
Written Warning
A formal write-up documenting the issue. You will be asked to review and acknowledge it here.
3️⃣
Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
A structured plan with specific goals and a timeline to address ongoing performance concerns.
4️⃣
Termination
Final step if prior warnings have not resulted in improvement, or for severe violations.
⚖️ Your Rights
💬
You can respond. If you receive a write-up, you have the right to submit a written response. Your response will be attached to the record and reviewed by HR.
🔍
You can dispute it. If you believe a write-up was issued unfairly or in error, contact HR immediately via the Messages tab. HR will review the situation.
🔒
It stays private. Write-ups are confidential HR records. They are not shared with other employees or outside the company.
📅
They can be cleared. Written warnings are typically reviewed after 6–12 months. Consistent good performance can result in a warning being cleared from your active record.
