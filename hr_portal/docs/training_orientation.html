1 Welcome to AI-Prowler

AI-Prowler is a technology company specializing in AI-powered business automation tools. Our flagship product — the AI-Prowler platform — helps small businesses manage jobs, invoices, scheduling, HR, and communications all in one place, powered by Claude AI.

We are a fully remote company. Every team member works from home, which means our culture, communication, and collaboration tools are built for a distributed team. This isn't a perk — it's how we operate, and we've built everything around making remote work as seamless and productive as possible.

2 Our Mission & Values

AI-Prowler's mission is to make powerful AI tools accessible to every small business owner — not just large enterprises. We believe that the right tools, thoughtfully built, can help everyday business owners save time, reduce errors, and grow confidently.

Our Core Values

  • 🤖 AI-First Thinking — We embrace AI as a collaborator, not just a tool. We use it to do our best work.
  • 🤝 Trust by Default — We trust our team to do great work without micromanagement. Transparency keeps us aligned.
  • 🚀 Bias Toward Action — We move fast, ship often, learn from feedback, and iterate quickly.
  • 🧩 Simplicity First — We build things that are easy to use. If it's complicated, it's not done yet.
  • ❤️ People Over Process — Our team and our customers are always more important than any policy or procedure.

5 Remote Work Expectations

Working remotely at AI-Prowler comes with freedom — and responsibility. Here's what we expect:

  • Be online during your agreed core hours. Your manager needs to be able to reach you.
  • Clock in and out every day using the Time Clock tab in the HR portal.
  • Respond to messages within 2 hours during your core hours.
  • Be camera-ready for scheduled video calls. A clean, quiet background is all we need.
  • Use company-approved tools for all work. Don't use personal accounts for work communications.
  • Notify your manager immediately if you'll be late or out — use the Running Late / Out Today buttons in Time Clock.
⏱ Pay is bi-weekly, every Friday via direct deposit. Set up your deposit info in the Deposits tab as soon as possible to ensure your first paycheck arrives on time.

7 Pay, Benefits & Time Off

Pay

  • Pay schedule: bi-weekly, every Friday via direct deposit
  • Pay stubs available in the Payroll tab the day before payday
  • Questions about pay? Message HR through the portal

Time Off

  • PTO: 15 days/year for full-time employees, accrues at 1.25 days per month
  • Sick Days: 5 days per calendar year
  • Personal Days: 2 days per calendar year
  • Holidays: 11 paid company holidays per year

Benefits

  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • All benefits details are available in the Pay & Benefits tab
