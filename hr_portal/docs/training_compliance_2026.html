1 2026 Policy Updates at AI-Prowler

Each year, AI-Prowler reviews and updates its policies to reflect changes in law, best practices, and company growth. Here are the key updates for 2026 that every employee needs to know:

🆕 Updated

Attendance & Punctuality Policy — Remote Work Standards

Core hour expectations have been clarified. All employees must be reachable within 2 hours during agreed core hours. The Running Late and Out Today quick-action buttons in the Time Clock tab are now the required notification method — texting your manager directly is no longer sufficient.

🆕 Updated

PTO Rollover Cap

Effective January 1, 2026, unused PTO rolls over to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days (80 hours). Any balance above 10 days at year-end will be forfeited. Plan your time off early to avoid losing earned PTO.

🆕 New

HR Portal — Mandatory for All Requests

Effective 2026, ALL time off requests, incident reports, IT support requests, and HR communications must go through the AI-Prowler HR Employee Portal. Requests made via personal text, personal email, or verbal agreement only are no longer accepted as official submissions.

🆕 Updated

Data Privacy & Remote Work

New guidance for 2026: employees must use company-approved tools for all work files. Personal cloud accounts (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) may not be used to store or share work files. All work must remain in company-approved systems.

3 Anti-Harassment & Discrimination Refresher

AI-Prowler maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination. As a refresher:

  • Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic (race, gender, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, etc.) that creates a hostile work environment
  • Sexual harassment includes unwelcome advances, inappropriate comments, and quid pro quo situations — all prohibited
  • Discrimination means treating someone less favorably in employment decisions because of a protected characteristic
  • Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited
🚨 If you experience or witness harassment or discrimination, report it immediately via the Messages tab or Incident Reports tab in the HR portal. All reports are confidential and protected.

4 Data Privacy & Confidentiality Refresher

Protecting company and client data is every employee's responsibility. Key reminders for 2026:

  • Never store work files on personal cloud accounts or personal devices without authorization
  • Lock your screen whenever you step away from your workspace
  • Do not share client names, data, or project details on social media
  • Report any suspected data breach immediately to HR — do not try to investigate on your own
  • Your NDA obligations continue after employment ends
🔒 A data breach must be reported within hours, not days. Every hour of delay increases potential harm. When in doubt, report it.

5 Workplace Safety Refresher

Quick reminders for maintaining a safe home office in 2026:

  • All work-related injuries must be reported via Incident Reports within 24 hours — even minor ones
  • Apply the 20-20-20 rule for eye health: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds
  • Take a movement break every 60-90 minutes to prevent ergonomic injury
  • Keep your workspace free of trip hazards, electrical risks, and fire hazards
  • Know your nearest emergency exit and the address of your nearest emergency room

7 Code of Conduct Refresher

A quick refresher on our professional standards heading into the rest of 2026:

  • Communication: Be clear, professional, and respectful in all written and verbal communication. Respond within 2 hours during core hours.
  • Confidentiality: Never share internal information, client data, or business strategies outside AI-Prowler without authorization.
  • Conflicts of interest: Disclose any relationship that could influence a business decision. When in doubt, tell HR.
  • Social media: Do not share confidential company information or make statements that could be interpreted as official AI-Prowler positions.
  • Substance use: Do not attend video calls or perform work duties while impaired.
✅ When in doubt about whether something is appropriate, ask HR via the Messages tab. It's always better to ask than to assume.
