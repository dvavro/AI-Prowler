Attendance & Punctuality Policy

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.2 Owner: HR Department

1. Purpose

AI-Prowler is a fully remote-first company. This policy establishes expectations for attendance, availability, and punctuality for all employees working from home. Our goal is to maintain a reliable, collaborative, and accountable team culture regardless of physical location.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of AI-Prowler who work in a remote or hybrid capacity.

3. Core Hours & Availability

All employees must be online, available, and responsive during their scheduled core hours. While AI-Prowler supports flexible scheduling, each employee must agree on their working hours with their manager at hire or during any schedule change.

⏱ Employees must be reachable via company-approved communication tools (email, portal messaging) during their agreed core hours.

4. Clocking In & Out

All employees must use the AI-Prowler HR Portal Time Clock to log daily hours for accurate payroll processing and project tracking.

  • Clock in at the start of your scheduled shift via the Time Clock tab in the employee portal.
  • Clock out at the end of each working day or when stepping away for an extended break.
  • If you forget to clock in or out, notify your manager or HR immediately to correct the record.
  • Consistent failure to clock in/out may result in payroll discrepancies and HR follow-up.

5. Tardiness

Tardiness is defined as beginning work more than 10 minutes after your agreed start time without prior notice.

  • If you will be late, notify your manager before your scheduled start time via the portal Running Late feature or direct message.
  • Three or more instances of unreported tardiness within a 30-day period will be documented and may result in disciplinary action.

6. Absences

All absences must be reported through the AI-Prowler HR Portal as early as possible.

7. Running Late or Out Today

The AI-Prowler HR Portal includes Running Late and Out Today quick-action buttons under the Time Clock section. Use these to instantly notify your manager and HR. Notifications are logged automatically in your record.

8. Remote Work Environment Standards

While working from home, all employees are expected to maintain a professional and productive environment:

  • Maintain a dedicated, distraction-minimized workspace during core hours.
  • Have a reliable internet connection adequate for video calls and portal access.
  • Be camera-ready for scheduled video meetings unless otherwise agreed with your manager.
  • Respond to messages and emails within 2 hours during your scheduled core hours.
  • Avoid personal errands or unsupervised childcare during scheduled hours without prior arrangement.

9. Time Zone & Scheduling

AI-Prowler operates primarily in Mountain Standard / Mountain Daylight Time (MST/MDT). Employees in other time zones must coordinate schedules with their manager to ensure sufficient overlap with team core hours. All meetings default to MST/MDT unless noted otherwise.

10. Consequences for Non-Compliance

Failure to adhere to this policy will be addressed progressively:

  1. Verbal Warning — First documented instance, logged in HR system.
  2. Written Warning — Continued pattern after verbal warning.
  3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) — Ongoing non-compliance.
  4. Termination — Failure to improve after PIP, or severe violation such as no-call no-show.

11. Questions & Exceptions

For questions or to request a temporary schedule exception, use the Messages tab in your AI-Prowler HR Portal to contact HR. All requests are confidential and reviewed promptly.
