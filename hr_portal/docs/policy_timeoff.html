Time Off & Leave Policy

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.1 Owner: HR Department

1. Purpose

AI-Prowler recognizes that time away from work is essential for employee health, well-being, and long-term productivity. This policy outlines the types of leave available, how to request time off, accrual rates, and important rules around leave usage for all remote employees.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of AI-Prowler. Contractors and freelancers are not eligible for paid leave benefits unless specifically stated in their contract.

4. PTO / Vacation Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days (10 hours) of PTO per month, totaling 15 days (120 hours) per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis based on their scheduled hours.

  • PTO begins accruing from your first day of employment.
  • PTO may be used after 90 days of employment (probationary period).
  • Requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the PTO & Time Off tab in the HR Portal.
  • PTO is subject to manager approval based on business needs and team coverage.
  • Unused PTO rolls over to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days (80 hours). Any balance above this cap will be forfeited.
📅 Plan your PTO early! Back-to-back requests from multiple team members may not all be approved. First come, first served based on submission date.

5. Sick Leave

All full-time employees receive 5 sick days (40 hours) per calendar year, granted at the start of each year (January 1st, or upon hire for new employees).

  • Sick leave may be used for your own illness, a medical appointment, or to care for an immediate family member who is ill.
  • Notify your manager and log the absence in the HR Portal before your scheduled start time whenever possible.
  • If sick for 3 or more consecutive days, a doctor's note may be requested by HR.
  • Unused sick days do not roll over to the next year and are not paid out upon termination.

6. Personal Days

Employees receive 2 personal days per calendar year to use for any reason — errands, rest, religious observances, or anything else. Personal days do not require explanation.

  • Give at least 24 hours notice when possible via the HR Portal.
  • Personal days do not roll over and are not paid out upon termination.

9. Jury Duty & Court Appearances

Employees summoned for jury duty or required to appear in court as a witness will receive full pay for the duration of their service, up to 2 weeks. Employees must notify HR immediately upon receiving a summons and provide a copy of the documentation via the portal.

10. Family & Medical Leave (FMLA)

AI-Prowler complies fully with the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying reasons including:

  • The birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
  • A serious health condition affecting the employee or an immediate family member.
  • Qualifying military exigency related to a spouse, child, or parent on active duty.

11. How to Request Time Off

All time-off requests must be submitted through the AI-Prowler HR Portal:

  1. Go to PTO & Time Off in the left sidebar of the employee portal.
  2. Select the leave type (Vacation, Sick, Personal, etc.).
  3. Enter your start and end dates and add any optional notes.
  4. Click Submit Request.
  5. You will be notified of approval or denial via the portal.
✅ Approved time off is automatically reflected in your schedule and the company-wide calendar so your team is always informed.

12. Unused Leave & Payout Upon Termination

  • PTO: Accrued, unused PTO is paid out upon separation in states that require it. Arizona does not mandate PTO payout; however, AI-Prowler may pay out a portion at management discretion.
  • Sick & Personal Days: Not paid out upon termination.
  • Negative PTO balance: Employees who have used more PTO than accrued at time of separation may have the negative balance deducted from their final paycheck.

13. Questions

For any questions about your leave balance, a pending request, or an exceptional situation not covered here, contact HR via the Messages tab in your AI-Prowler HR Portal. All conversations are confidential.
