AI-Prowler · Human Resources

Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.0 Owner: HR & Legal Department

1. Purpose

AI-Prowler is committed to protecting the privacy of its employees, clients, and partners, and maintaining the confidentiality of all sensitive business information. As a remote company, our data lives in digital systems and travels across home networks — which makes a clear, practical privacy policy essential for everyone on the team.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all AI-Prowler employees, contractors, and vendors who have access to company systems, client data, or any proprietary information — regardless of their location or device.

3. What Counts as Confidential Information

The following categories of information are considered confidential and must be protected at all times:

CategoryExamples
Business InformationFinancials, contracts, pricing, business strategies, product roadmaps
Client & Customer DataNames, contact info, account details, usage data, communications
Employee DataSalaries, performance reviews, HR records, personal contact info
Technical / IPSource code, AI models, system architecture, proprietary algorithms
Internal CommunicationsInternal emails, meeting notes, HR discussions, management decisions
Vendor & Partner InfoPartner agreements, vendor pricing, negotiation details
🔒 When in doubt, treat it as confidential. If you are unsure whether information can be shared, ask HR or your manager before sharing it.

4. Employee Privacy Rights

AI-Prowler respects the personal privacy of its employees. We collect only the information necessary to manage employment and operate the business. Employee data is used for:

  • Payroll processing and tax reporting
  • Benefits administration
  • Performance management and HR records
  • Emergency contact purposes
  • Compliance with applicable laws
👤 Employee personal data is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or disclosed without consent except as required by law.

5. Handling & Storing Confidential Data

All employees who handle confidential information — which includes almost everyone at AI-Prowler — must follow these practices:

  • Store all work files in company-approved systems only (AI-Prowler platforms, approved cloud tools). Do not save work files to personal accounts, personal hard drives, or unapproved apps.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for all work accounts and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available.
  • Lock your computer screen whenever you step away — even at home.
  • Do not work on confidential matters in public locations (coffee shops, libraries, coworking spaces) unless using a VPN and a privacy screen.
  • Shred or securely dispose of any printed confidential documents. Do not leave sensitive printouts in shared areas of your home.
  • Do not take screenshots or photographs of confidential screens and share them outside of authorized work channels.

6. Client Data Protection

Client trust is the foundation of AI-Prowler's business. All client data must be treated with the highest level of care:

  • Access only the client data you need to complete your specific job duties — do not browse or pull client records without a work reason.
  • Never share client data with any third party, contractor, or other client without explicit written authorization.
  • Report any suspected unauthorized access to client data to HR and management immediately.
  • Client data must be deleted or returned when no longer needed for a project, per the terms of the client agreement.
🚨 A client data breach is a critical incident. If you suspect one, report it immediately — do not wait to investigate on your own.

7. Non-Disclosure Obligations

All AI-Prowler employees sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as part of their onboarding. Key obligations include:

  • You may not disclose confidential company information to anyone outside AI-Prowler during your employment or after it ends.
  • You may not use confidential information for personal gain or to benefit any other business.
  • NDA obligations survive your employment — they do not end when you leave the company.
  • If you receive a legal request (subpoena, court order) for company information, notify HR immediately before responding.
📝 If you have not yet signed your NDA or cannot locate your copy, contact HR through the portal Messages tab right away.

8. Company Systems & Monitoring

AI-Prowler may monitor activity on company-provided systems and networks to ensure security and policy compliance. This includes:

  • Access logs for company platforms and applications
  • Email and communication metadata on company accounts
  • Activity on company-issued devices
🔍 Monitoring is used for security purposes only — not to micromanage employees. AI-Prowler values your privacy and will only review detailed logs when there is a legitimate security or compliance reason.

9. Social Media & Public Disclosure

  • Do not share confidential company information, client names, project details, or internal communications on any social media platform.
  • Do not post screenshots of internal tools, dashboards, or portals publicly.
  • If a journalist, researcher, or outside party contacts you about AI-Prowler, direct them to management before making any statements.

10. Data Breach Response

If you believe company or client data has been accessed, stolen, or exposed:

  1. Stop using the affected system or account immediately.
  2. Report it to HR and your manager right away via the portal Messages tab or phone.
  3. Do not attempt to investigate or fix the issue on your own.
  4. Preserve any evidence — do not delete emails, logs, or files related to the incident.
  5. HR and management will coordinate the response, including notifying affected clients if required.
⚡ Time matters in a data breach. Every hour of delay increases potential damage. Report it immediately.

11. Post-Employment Obligations

When your employment with AI-Prowler ends, your privacy and confidentiality obligations continue:

  • Return all company equipment, devices, and access credentials on your last day.
  • Delete all company files from any personal devices where they were stored.
  • Your NDA remains in effect — you may not disclose or use confidential information in any future role.
  • Access to all AI-Prowler systems will be revoked on your last day of employment.

12. Violations

Violations of this policy — including accidental disclosures — must be reported immediately. Depending on severity, consequences may include:

  • Written warning and mandatory retraining
  • Suspension pending investigation
  • Termination of employment
  • Legal action in cases involving intentional disclosure or NDA breach

13. Questions

If you have questions about what can and cannot be shared, or you need to report a concern, use the Messages tab in your AI-Prowler HR Portal. All communications are confidential.
