1 Welcome to the AI-Prowler Platform

AI-Prowler is the platform that powers your entire work life at our company. It's where you clock in, request time off, view your pay, communicate with HR, and complete trainings — all in one place, from any device.

The platform has two sides:

  • Employee Portal (where you are now) — your personal dashboard for everything work-related
  • HR Admin App — used by HR and managers to manage the team, view your records, and respond to requests
📱 The Employee Portal works on your phone too! Open it in your mobile browser and tap Add to Home Screen to bookmark it like an app.

3 Time Clock — Clocking In & Out

The Time Clock is the most important tool you'll use every day. Your clock-ins and clock-outs are sent directly to HR in real time for payroll processing.

1

Click Time Clock in the sidebar under Workspace

2

Press the green Clock In button at the start of your shift

3

Press Clock Out when you're done for the day

4

If you're running late, tap Running Late to instantly notify your manager

5

If you can't work today, tap Out Today to notify HR automatically

⚠️ Always clock in at the start of your shift and clock out at the end. Missed clock-ins cause payroll issues. If you forget, contact HR immediately via the Messages tab.

4 Requesting Time Off

All time off requests go through the portal — never through personal text or email. This keeps everything documented and ensures fast approval.

1

Click PTO / Time Off in the sidebar under Time Off

2

Click Request Time Off

3

Select the type: Vacation, Sick, Personal, or Other

4

Enter your start and end dates

5

Add a note (optional) and click Submit Request

✅ You'll be notified of approval or denial through the portal. Approved time off automatically shows on the Company Calendar so your team is always informed.

5 Messaging HR

The Messages tab is your private, confidential line to HR. Use it for questions about pay, policies, schedules, concerns, or anything else you need HR's help with.

1

Click Messages in the sidebar under Company

2

Type your subject and message in the compose box

3

Click Send Message to HR

4

HR will reply in the same Messages tab — you'll see their response there

🔒 All messages to HR are private and confidential. Only HR staff can see them — not your manager or coworkers.

6 Viewing Your Pay

💰
Payroll
Sidebar → Money → Payroll

View your pay stubs, earnings history, tax withholdings, and deductions. Pay stubs are available the business day before every payday (bi-weekly Fridays).

🏦
Direct Deposit
Sidebar → Money → Deposits

Set up or update your direct deposit bank account. Do this as soon as possible after starting to ensure your first paycheck arrives on time.

💳
Pay & Benefits
Sidebar → Workspace → Pay & Benefits

View your benefits enrollment, health insurance details, and compensation information.

7 Filing an Incident Report

If you experience a work-related injury, near-miss, or safety concern, file an Incident Report immediately. All reports are confidential.

1

Click Incident Reports in the sidebar under HR

2

Click + New Incident Report

3

Fill in all required sections — incident type, location, description, witnesses

4

Add your digital signature and click Submit

5

Your report is sent directly to HR. You can track its status in the Incident Reports tab.
