Compensation & Pay Policy

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.1 Owner: HR & Finance Department

1. Purpose

This policy outlines AI-Prowler's compensation philosophy, pay schedules, overtime practices, and raise and bonus procedures. AI-Prowler is committed to providing fair, competitive, and transparent compensation to all employees in our fully remote work environment.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of AI-Prowler. Contractor compensation terms are governed separately by individual contract agreements, though general principles outlined here still apply.

3. Pay Types

AI-Prowler uses the following compensation structures depending on the role and agreement:

Pay TypeDescriptionWho It Applies To
HourlyPaid per hour worked, tracked via the HR Portal Time ClockPart-time & hourly employees
SalaryFixed annual compensation divided into regular pay periodsFull-time salaried employees
Contract / ProjectFixed fee per project or deliverable as outlined in contractContractors & freelancers
💰 Your pay type and rate are outlined in your offer letter or employment agreement. Contact HR via the portal if you have questions about your classification.

4. Pay Schedule

AI-Prowler processes payroll on a bi-weekly schedule (every two weeks). Payday falls on Friday. If a payday falls on a bank holiday, payment will be processed on the preceding business day.

Pay PeriodPay DateMethod
Sunday – Saturday (2 weeks)Following FridayDirect Deposit
Contractors (project-based)Per contract termsDirect Deposit or agreed method
✅ All employees are paid via direct deposit. You can set up or update your direct deposit information at any time in the Deposits tab of your HR Portal.

5. Timekeeping & Pay Accuracy

Accurate timekeeping is essential for correct payroll processing. All hourly employees must:

  • Clock in and out using the Time Clock tab in the AI-Prowler HR Portal every working day.
  • Review their logged hours within 24 hours of each shift for accuracy.
  • Report any discrepancies to their manager or HR immediately — corrections must be submitted before the payroll cutoff (Wednesday noon of the pay week).
  • Never clock in or out on behalf of another employee.
⚠ Falsification of time records is grounds for immediate termination.

6. Overtime

AI-Prowler complies with all applicable federal and state overtime laws.

  • Non-exempt (hourly) employees are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate for all hours worked over 40 in a workweek.
  • Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager in writing (via the portal or email) before the hours are worked.
  • Salaried exempt employees are not entitled to overtime pay under federal law, per their classification.
  • Unauthorized overtime may still be compensated but will result in a formal conversation with management.

7. Pay Stubs & Pay History

Employees can view their pay stubs, earnings history, and deduction details at any time through the Payroll tab in the AI-Prowler HR Portal. Pay stubs are made available the business day before each payday.

  • Pay stubs include: gross pay, federal and state tax withholdings, any benefit deductions, and net pay.
  • Employees are responsible for reviewing each pay stub and reporting discrepancies to HR within 5 business days of receipt.

8. Deductions

The following deductions may appear on your paycheck:

  • Mandatory deductions: Federal income tax, state income tax (if applicable), Social Security (6.2%), and Medicare (1.45%).
  • Voluntary deductions: Health, dental, and vision insurance premiums; retirement contributions; and any other elected benefit deductions.

Questions about deductions should be directed to HR via the portal Messages tab.

9. Pay Raises & Salary Reviews

AI-Prowler conducts formal compensation reviews on an annual basis, typically in Q1 each calendar year. Merit-based raises are awarded based on performance, role growth, and company financial health.

  • Annual performance reviews are conducted through the HR portal or via manager review meetings.
  • Raises, if awarded, take effect at the start of the next full pay period following approval.
  • Market adjustments may be made outside of the annual cycle when necessary to remain competitive.
  • Off-cycle raises require VP or executive approval and are coordinated through HR.
🌟 Employees who take on expanded responsibilities, demonstrate exceptional performance, or earn new certifications are encouraged to discuss compensation adjustments with their manager at any time.

10. Bonuses

AI-Prowler may offer discretionary bonuses based on individual performance, team results, or company-wide milestones. Bonuses are not guaranteed and are at the discretion of management and executive leadership.

  • Performance bonuses are communicated in writing and paid out in the next regular payroll cycle after approval.
  • All bonuses are subject to standard tax withholdings.
  • Referral bonuses, project completion bonuses, and other incentive programs will be communicated separately by HR when applicable.

11. Pay Equity & Non-Discrimination

AI-Prowler is committed to pay equity. Compensation decisions are made without regard to race, color, gender, gender identity, age, national origin, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by law. If you believe there is a pay equity issue, please contact HR confidentially through the portal Messages tab.

12. Garnishments & Wage Orders

AI-Prowler complies with all legally required wage garnishment orders (e.g. child support, tax levies). If you receive a garnishment order or have questions about one, contact HR immediately and in confidence.

13. Questions & Pay Disputes

If you believe there is an error in your pay, take the following steps:

  1. Review your pay stub in the Payroll tab of the HR Portal.
  2. Message HR directly via the portal Messages tab with your concern and pay period in question.
  3. HR will investigate and, if an error is confirmed, issue a correction in the next pay cycle or sooner if the amount is significant.
