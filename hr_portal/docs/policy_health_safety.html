Health & Safety Policy

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.0 Owner: HR Department

1. Purpose

AI-Prowler is committed to the health, safety, and well-being of every employee — even though our team works remotely from home. While traditional workplace hazards are reduced in a home office environment, new risks exist around ergonomics, digital well-being, mental health, and home workspace safety. This policy outlines our expectations and the support AI-Prowler provides to keep every team member safe and healthy.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all AI-Prowler employees working from home or any remote location. It covers physical workspace safety, ergonomics, mental health, emergency procedures, and incident reporting.

3. Home Workspace Safety

Your home workspace is your workplace. While AI-Prowler cannot inspect your home, we ask all employees to take reasonable steps to ensure their work area is safe:

  • Keep your workspace clear of clutter, trip hazards, and obstructions.
  • Ensure electrical cords and cables are properly managed and not a trip or fire hazard.
  • Work in a well-lit area to reduce eye strain and headaches.
  • Make sure your home has working smoke detectors and a fire extinguisher accessible.
  • Ensure your internet router and any company equipment are on a surge protector.
  • Do not eat or drink near company equipment to prevent damage or electrical hazards.
  • Keep pets and children away from your workspace during working hours when possible to prevent accidents and distractions.
🏠 A safe home workspace protects both you and your equipment. If you need help setting up a safe work area, contact HR — we may be able to assist with equipment or resources.

4. Ergonomics

Ergonomic injuries are one of the most common health issues for remote workers. Poor posture, bad monitor placement, and uncomfortable seating can cause lasting physical problems. AI-Prowler encourages every employee to set up their workspace ergonomically:

AreaRecommendation
ChairAdjustable height so feet rest flat on the floor; lumbar support for lower back
Desk / SurfaceElbows at 90° when typing; wrists straight and not bent
MonitorTop of screen at or slightly below eye level; arm's length away from face
Keyboard & MouseAt elbow height; use a wrist rest if needed
LightingAvoid glare on screen; natural light from the side, not directly behind or in front
Laptop UseUse an external monitor or laptop stand + external keyboard if working long hours
💻 If you are experiencing pain or discomfort related to your home workstation setup, contact HR. AI-Prowler may be able to provide ergonomic equipment to support you.

5. Breaks & Rest

Working from home can make it easy to forget to take breaks — or conversely, to overwork. Both extremes are harmful. AI-Prowler encourages healthy work rhythms:

  • Take a short break (5–10 minutes) every 60–90 minutes to stand, stretch, and rest your eyes.
  • Use the 20-20-20 rule for eye strain: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
  • Take a proper lunch break away from your screen — at least 30 minutes.
  • Avoid working significantly beyond your scheduled hours on a regular basis. If workload is consistently requiring overtime, discuss it with your manager.
  • Disconnect from work communications after your scheduled hours. You are not expected to be available 24/7.

6. Mental Health & Well-Being

Remote work can be isolating, and AI-Prowler takes employee mental health seriously. We encourage a culture where it is safe to talk about how you are doing.

  • If you are struggling with stress, burnout, anxiety, or other mental health challenges, please reach out to HR through the portal Messages tab. All conversations are confidential.
  • Take your PTO. Rest is productive. AI-Prowler actively encourages employees to use their earned time off.
  • Participate in team check-ins and virtual social events — connection matters even when we are remote.
  • Managers are trained to check in on team members regularly, not just about work output but about overall well-being.
💕 If you are ever in crisis or need immediate mental health support, please contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988. You do not have to face it alone.

7. Cybersecurity & Digital Safety

Working from home means working on networks outside of a traditional secure office. Digital safety is a key part of health and safety for AI-Prowler employees:

  • Use a secure, password-protected home Wi-Fi network. Do not work on public or unsecured networks without a VPN.
  • Keep your operating system, browser, and security software up to date.
  • Do not click suspicious links or download attachments from unknown senders — report phishing attempts to HR immediately.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all work accounts.
  • Lock your screen when stepping away, even at home, especially if others share your living space.

8. Incident Reporting

Even in a remote work environment, incidents can happen — a slip or fall, an electrical issue, a repetitive strain injury, or a health event that occurred while working. All work-related incidents must be reported.

  • Report any injury or illness that occurs during work hours or is related to your work duties to HR immediately via the Incident Reports tab in the employee portal.
  • Include the date, time, what happened, and any immediate actions taken.
  • Seek medical attention if needed — your health comes first.
  • AI-Prowler carries workers' compensation insurance. Work-related injuries occurring during scheduled work hours may be covered regardless of your location.
🚨 Do not delay reporting a work-related injury. Timely reporting protects you legally and ensures you get the support you need. File via the Incident Reports tab in your HR portal.

9. Emergency Procedures

In any emergency — medical, fire, natural disaster, or other — your safety comes first. No work task is worth risking your life.

  • Call 911 immediately for any life-threatening emergency.
  • Notify your manager or HR as soon as you are safe — via the portal, email, or phone.
  • Know the location of your nearest emergency exit from your home workspace.
  • Have a basic first aid kit accessible in your home.
  • Know the address of your nearest emergency room or urgent care facility.
🚑 Emergency contacts: Call 911 for all emergencies. For non-emergency work-related concerns, use the HR Portal Messages tab or contact your manager directly.

10. Domestic Violence & Personal Safety

AI-Prowler recognizes that home is not always a safe space for everyone. If you are experiencing domestic violence, abuse, or any personal safety threat:

  • Contact HR confidentially through the portal Messages tab — we will work with you to adjust your schedule, contact information visibility, or other accommodations.
  • The National Domestic Violence Hotline is available 24/7: 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788.
  • AI-Prowler will never share your personal address or contact information without your explicit consent.

11. Accommodations

AI-Prowler will provide reasonable accommodations to employees with disabilities or health conditions that affect their ability to work. To request an accommodation:

  1. Contact HR through the portal Messages tab describing your need (you do not need to disclose your diagnosis).
  2. HR will work with you and your manager to identify appropriate accommodations.
  3. All accommodation requests are handled confidentially.

12. Questions

For any health or safety concern — large or small — use the Messages tab in your AI-Prowler HR Portal to reach HR. We are here to help, not to judge.
