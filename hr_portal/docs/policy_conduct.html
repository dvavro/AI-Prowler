AI-Prowler · Human Resources

Code of Conduct

Remote / Work-From-Home
Effective: January 1, 2026 Updated: August 2026 Version: 1.0 Owner: HR Department

1. Purpose

AI-Prowler is built on a foundation of trust, respect, and professionalism. Even as a fully remote company, we hold ourselves to a high standard of conduct in every interaction — whether it's an email, a video call, a portal message, or a team chat. This Code of Conduct exists not to restrict anyone, but to protect everyone and ensure AI-Prowler remains a great place to work.

💡 We understand that working from home comes with its own unique environment. This policy is designed to be practical and fair for remote workers, not to police your personal space.

2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all AI-Prowler employees, contractors, and vendors in all work-related settings, including but not limited to:

  • All written and verbal communication (email, Slack, portal messaging, text)
  • Video calls, virtual meetings, and conference calls
  • Social media when representing AI-Prowler or identifiable as an AI-Prowler employee
  • Any client, partner, or vendor interaction
  • Company-sponsored events, virtual or in-person

3. Professional Conduct & Communication

AI-Prowler expects all team members to communicate professionally, clearly, and respectfully at all times. Working remotely means that most of our communication is written, which makes tone and clarity especially important.

  • Be clear, concise, and professional in all written communications.
  • Respond to messages from managers and teammates within a reasonable time during your core hours (generally within 2 hours).
  • Avoid using aggressive, sarcastic, or demeaning language in any work communication.
  • Use company-approved tools for all work-related communication — do not use personal accounts for company business.
  • Be on time for all scheduled video calls and meetings. If you cannot attend, notify the organizer in advance.
  • On video calls, maintain a reasonably professional appearance and background when your camera is on.
📷 You do not need a professional studio setup for video calls. A clean, reasonably quiet space is all that is expected.

4. Respect & Inclusion

AI-Prowler is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free workplace for everyone — regardless of race, color, gender, gender identity, age, religion, national origin, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

  • Treat every colleague, client, and vendor with dignity and respect.
  • Do not make jokes, comments, or statements that demean, stereotype, or exclude any individual or group.
  • Respect differences in communication styles, working habits, and time zones.
  • Actively support an inclusive team culture where everyone feels heard and valued.
🚫 Harassment of any kind — including verbal, written, or visual — will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination. This includes behavior on video calls, in chats, and on social media.

5. Harassment & Discrimination Policy

AI-Prowler has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Sexual harassment — unwelcome advances, inappropriate comments, or sharing explicit content
  • Bullying — repeated hostile, demeaning, or intimidating behavior toward a colleague
  • Discrimination — treating someone unfairly based on a protected characteristic
  • Retaliation — punishing someone for reporting harassment or participating in an investigation

If you experience or witness any of the above, report it immediately to HR through the Messages tab in the employee portal. All reports are confidential. No one will face retaliation for making a good-faith report.

6. Dress Code — Remote Workers

AI-Prowler does not enforce a strict dress code for day-to-day remote work. You are working from home, and we trust you to dress comfortably and appropriately for your environment. However:

  • When attending video calls with clients, partners, or leadership, dress in a manner that represents AI-Prowler professionally (business casual at minimum).
  • For internal team video calls, use your best judgment — neat and presentable is all we ask.
  • Avoid appearing on camera in sleepwear, swimwear, or any attire that could be considered offensive or inappropriate.
👍 No suit required. We get it — you're at home. Just look like you meant to show up.

7. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict — or appear to conflict — with the interests of AI-Prowler.

  • Do not work for a direct competitor of AI-Prowler without prior written approval from management.
  • Do not use AI-Prowler resources, time, or proprietary information for personal business or side projects without approval.
  • Disclose any personal relationship with a vendor, client, or job candidate that could influence a business decision.
  • Do not accept gifts, payments, or favors from vendors or clients that could influence your professional judgment.

When in doubt, disclose it to HR. Undisclosed conflicts of interest may result in disciplinary action.

8. Confidentiality & Data Privacy

As a remote employee, you handle company and client data from your home environment. Protecting that data is a serious responsibility.

  • Do not share company data, client information, or proprietary materials with anyone outside of AI-Prowler without authorization.
  • Use company-approved software and tools only. Do not store work files on personal cloud accounts (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
  • Lock your screen and secure your workspace when stepping away, especially if others are present in your home.
  • Do not discuss confidential company matters in public spaces (coffee shops, shared offices) where you can be overheard.
  • Report any suspected data breach or security incident to HR and your manager immediately.
🔒 Sharing confidential company or client information with unauthorized parties is a serious violation and grounds for immediate termination.

9. Social Media & Public Representation

Employees are free to use personal social media and are not required to represent AI-Prowler publicly. However, when you are identifiable as an AI-Prowler employee:

  • Do not share confidential company information, client names, or proprietary work on social media.
  • Do not make statements that could be interpreted as official AI-Prowler positions unless you are authorized to do so.
  • Be mindful that public statements made while affiliated with AI-Prowler can reflect on the company and your colleagues.
  • Treat AI-Prowler colleagues, clients, and partners with respect even in public online spaces.

10. Use of Company Resources

AI-Prowler may provide employees with software licenses, equipment, or other resources to perform their jobs. These resources are for work use.

  • Use company-provided tools and software for work purposes only.
  • Do not install unauthorized software on company-provided devices.
  • Report lost, stolen, or damaged company equipment to HR and your manager immediately.
  • Return all company equipment promptly upon separation from the company.

11. Substance Use

AI-Prowler employees are expected to be fit for work during their scheduled hours. Because we are a remote company, we understand you are in your own home — however:

  • Do not attend video calls or perform work duties while impaired by alcohol, drugs, or any substance that affects your judgment or performance.
  • If you are on prescribed medication that may affect your ability to work safely, notify HR confidentially.

12. Reporting Violations

AI-Prowler encourages all employees to speak up when they witness or experience a violation of this Code. You will never face retaliation for making a good-faith report.

To report a concern, use one of the following:

  • Messages tab in the AI-Prowler HR Portal — direct, confidential message to HR
  • Incident Report under the HR section of the portal — for formal written documentation
  • Direct email to HR if you prefer not to use the portal
✅ All reports are treated with confidentiality. HR will investigate promptly and fairly. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this Code.

13. Disciplinary Process

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be reviewed by HR and addressed based on severity:

SeverityExamplesTypical Response
MinorLate to calls, unprofessional tone in chatVerbal or written warning
ModerateRepeated minor violations, inappropriate social media postWritten warning, PIP
SeriousHarassment, conflict of interest, data breachSuspension, termination
SevereDiscrimination, sharing confidential data, violenceImmediate termination

AI-Prowler reserves the right to skip steps in the progressive discipline process if the severity of the violation warrants it.

14. Acknowledgment

All employees are required to acknowledge this Code of Conduct upon hire and whenever a significant update is made. By working at AI-Prowler you agree to uphold these standards. If you have questions about any section of this policy, contact HR through the portal Messages tab.
