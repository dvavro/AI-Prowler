1 Who We Are

AI-Prowler is a technology company that builds AI-powered business automation tools for small and medium-sized businesses. We are headquartered in Chandler, Arizona and operate as a fully remote company — every member of our team works from home.

Our platform is powered by Claude AI (built by Anthropic) and connects with dozens of tools, services, and workflows that small business owners use every day. We believe that AI shouldn't just be for large corporations with big IT budgets — it should be accessible, practical, and transformative for everyone.

2 Our Mission

"To make powerful AI tools accessible to every small business owner — not just large enterprises."

Small business owners are some of the hardest-working people in the world. They handle sales, operations, HR, customer service, finances, and marketing — often all on the same day. AI-Prowler exists to give them back time, reduce errors, and help them grow with confidence.

We're not building AI for AI's sake. Every feature we build has to answer one question: does this make a real difference for a real business owner? If the answer is no, we don't build it.

3 Our Vision

Our vision is a future where any small business — a one-person landscaping company, a family-owned restaurant, a freelance graphic designer — has access to the same quality of intelligent business tools as a Fortune 500 company.

We believe AI will be as fundamental to business operations as email or spreadsheets are today. We want AI-Prowler to be the platform that gets small businesses there first, safely, and affordably.

🚀 We're not just building software — we're helping small business owners reclaim their time and grow their dreams. That's what we show up for every day.

5 Our Products

AI-Prowler is a platform — a collection of connected tools that work together. Here's what we build:

🏢

AI-Prowler Core Platform

The central hub. Handles job tracking, invoicing, scheduling, client management, and AI-powered automation for service-based small businesses. Connects to dozens of external tools via MCP (Model Context Protocol).

👥

AI-Prowler HR Suite

The HR platform you're using right now. Includes the Employee Portal (this app) and the HR Admin app. Handles onboarding, attendance, payroll, time off, trainings, incident reports, and employee documents.

📱

AI-Prowler PWA Apps

Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) that work like native mobile apps but run in the browser. No app store required. Employees and business owners can add them to their home screen for instant access.

🤖

AI-Prowler AI Assistant

Powered by Claude (Anthropic). Embedded throughout our products to answer questions, automate tasks, draft communications, analyze data, and help users get more done in less time.

6 Our Technology — What Powers AI-Prowler

Understanding our tech stack helps you understand what we build and why it matters:

  • Claude AI (Anthropic): The AI brain behind AI-Prowler. Claude handles conversations, document generation, analysis, and automation across all our products.
  • MCP (Model Context Protocol): An open standard that lets Claude connect to external tools and services — think of it as a universal translator between AI and business software.
  • Python: Our backend server language. Powers all API endpoints, data processing, and business logic.
  • HTML/CSS/JavaScript: Our frontend. All our apps are built as web applications that work on any device with a browser.
  • Cloudflare: Our cloud and security layer. Routes traffic, provides DDoS protection, and powers our remote access tunneling.
  • ChromaDB: Our vector database for AI memory and knowledge retrieval. Stores and searches documents intelligently.
💡 You don't need to be a developer to work at AI-Prowler — but understanding what these tools do helps you talk about our products intelligently with customers and colleagues.

7 Our Customers

AI-Prowler's primary customers are small and medium-sized business owners — specifically those in service-based industries:

  • Landscaping, lawn care, and outdoor services
  • Home services (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning)
  • Freelancers and independent contractors
  • Small retail and e-commerce businesses
  • Health and wellness practitioners
  • Restaurants and food service businesses

These customers typically have 1-50 employees and need tools that are affordable, easy to use, and powerful enough to replace multiple separate apps.

8 Our Remote Culture

Being fully remote is a core part of who we are — not just a perk or a policy. We've built our entire company from the ground up as a remote organization, which means:

  • Our tools and processes are designed for async communication
  • Documentation is taken seriously — if it's not written down, it doesn't exist
  • We trust team members to manage their own time and deliver results
  • We invest in making remote work feel connected, not isolated
  • Geography doesn't determine opportunity — talent does
🏠 Working remotely at AI-Prowler isn't just where you work — it's how we think. Our products are built for remote and distributed businesses because we ARE a remote and distributed business.

9 Where We're Going — Our Roadmap Vision

We're building toward a future where AI-Prowler is the all-in-one operating system for small businesses. Key areas we're expanding:

  • More industry-specific tools: Deeper functionality for specific verticals like landscaping, home services, and food service
  • Multi-user and team features: Expanding from solo business owners to teams with multiple employees and roles
  • Deeper AI automation: Moving from AI that answers questions to AI that takes actions — scheduling, invoicing, customer follow-up — automatically
  • Mobile-first experiences: Better PWA and native mobile experiences for business owners who live on their phone
  • Partner integrations: Connecting AI-Prowler with the accounting, CRM, and payroll tools our customers already use
