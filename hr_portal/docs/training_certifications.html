🎖 Certifications & Licenses

Understand which certifications and licenses apply to your role, how to keep them current, and how to submit proof to HR so your record stays accurate and compliant.

1 Why Certifications & Licenses Matter

Depending on your role, you may be required to hold a professional license, a safety certification, or a platform/technical certification to legally or safely perform your job. Keeping these current isn't just a formality — for regulated roles, working with an expired license or certification can create real legal and safety risk for you and for AI-Prowler.

🎖 A certification on file with HR is only useful if it's current. An expired certification is treated the same as having none at all.

3 Your Responsibility

Employees are responsible for knowing which certifications or licenses their role requires, keeping them active, and notifying HR before one lapses — not after.

  • Know your renewal date and calendar it well in advance
  • Renew before expiration, not after — many certifications take weeks to process
  • Notify HR immediately if a required certification is at risk of lapsing
  • Never perform work that legally requires a certification you don't currently hold

4 Submitting Proof to HR

Once you complete or renew a certification, upload a copy to your HR profile so it's on file:

  1. Go to your Documents tab in the employee portal
  2. Select the Certifications category
  3. Upload a clear copy of the certificate or license, including the issue and expiration dates
  4. HR will confirm receipt and file it under your Certifications portfolio
📎 Always include the expiration date on what you upload — HR can't track a renewal deadline it can't see.

6 What Happens If a Certification Lapses

If a certification required for your role expires, you may be temporarily reassigned away from tasks that require it until it's renewed. For safety-critical certifications, this is non-negotiable — it protects you as much as anyone else.

⚠️ Continuing to perform certification-required work after it has lapsed is a serious policy violation and, in some cases, a legal liability issue. If you're ever unsure whether your certification is still valid, ask HR before proceeding, not after.

7 Getting Support for a New Certification

If your role would benefit from a certification you don't currently hold, talk to your manager or HR. AI-Prowler may be able to support the cost or time needed, depending on the role and certification.

✅ Growing your certifications is one of the best ways to grow your career here — HR would rather help you get certified than find out later you needed one.
